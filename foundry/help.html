Quick start

Nine answers, one pack

About five minutes. Every answer can be a plain “don’t know” where noted.

1 · Instant Build

Open Instant Build and answer the nine questions:

  1. What you are building or operating. A sentence or two in your own words.
  2. Stage. Idea, pre-launch, launched or existing.
  3. Location. ZIP code or city, or “not decided”.
  4. Customer. Who it is for.
  5. Revenue model. How it makes money, or “unknown”.
  6. Personal capital. What you can put in yourself now: an amount, “$0”, “unknown” or “prefer not to say”. Not the launch cost; Foundry estimates that separately.
  7. Outside money. “None”, “unsure”, or what you would look for.
  8. Brand. New, import one you have, or decide later.
  9. When you want to open. “In about 3 months” is already chosen; pick “As soon as possible” or a date if you know it. Your timeline then uses real calendar dates. Not asked once a business is already open.

If the business is already launched or existing, you will also be asked for its legal or public name and whether it is formed. Current monthly numbers are optional and make the plan start from where the business is today.

The Optional detail section adds grant, loan and incubator paths and extra documents only if you pick them. Then tap Build documents.

2 · Guided Interview

After Instant Build, the Guided Interview on the Documents page makes your plan stronger: a conversation of about 60 to 90 minutes about your goals, your neighborhood, your customers and competitors, what you sell and what it costs, your money, team, suppliers, equipment, marketing, licenses and insurance. Your answers replace estimates throughout your documents. Every question can be skipped or answered “I don’t know yet”, which puts it on your To-Do list. Stop whenever you like and come back days later; your answers wait on this device and are removed after 30 days of no use, like your plan.

Your business plan information stays on this device. Foundry doesn’t send it to MaterialLogix or anyone else, and it’s never used to train anything. You can delete it at any time.

The questions can be read aloud in the MaterialLogix house voice; the audio is part of Foundry and plays from your device, and the words are always on screen. Turn it off in the interview’s Settings. If a local model is set up (the one Ask about this plan uses), the interviewer can also respond to your answers, ask a follow-up in your own words, and pick out figures for you to confirm; it runs on your device only, and the interview works the same without it. In the phone app you can answer by speaking where your phone recognizes speech on the device itself; in a browser or on desktop you type, because browser speech recognition can send your voice to an outside service.

The deeper questions are also available a few at a time under Complete your plan on the Documents page.

2b · Answer the questions yourself

Prefer to fill in a form instead of a conversation? Answer the questions yourself, also on the Documents page, is the same questions as the Guided Interview, laid out as chapters you can open in any order, with every question visible and answerable at once. Skip anything, or mark it “I don’t know yet”, the same way, onto the same To-Do list. Typing saves automatically, with a status line that says when it was last saved on this device.

The two are the same answers underneath: start in the Guided Interview and finish in the form, or the other way round, and nothing is lost either way. Either one can rebuild your documents.

3 · Bring what you already have

Choose files on Instant Build accepts plans, decks, spreadsheets, PDFs, Word files, notes and photos of paperwork. In this version Foundry notes which files you picked but does not read them yet, so your typed answers are what build the pack. Files never leave your device.

What you get

Your documents

All of them open, and can be edited, on the Documents page.

  • Read This First. Page one: notices for your location and what the pack is and is not.
  • Business Plan, Financial Statements, Local Licenses and a To-Do List. The core set, built every time.
  • Operating Plan. Your step-by-step manual for opening and running the business: a dated week-by-week launch timeline, state registrations with office, form, fee and link, start-up costs checked against published prices, suppliers, pricing worksheets, hiring, daily procedures, marketing, money management, weekly numbers and what to do when things go wrong. It is written to you and stays with you; anything the research could not confirm is marked To-Do rather than guessed. Built on this device from research packs that ship with the app.
  • Draft Notes. Optional. Tap Adjust notes to clarify anything that looks off; your documents update when you return to them.
  • Extras you chose, such as a pitch one-pager, grant readiness or a lender checklist.

The Plan readiness card shows how much of a lender-ready plan is answered. Complete your plan asks the missing questions a few at a time; anything left blank stays listed as missing rather than being made up.

Take it with you

Download & export

Each button works on the document that is open.

  • Export editable. Downloads the document as a file you can open in any browser, or edit in Word or Google Docs.
  • Archive. Downloads a finished offline copy to keep.
  • Save as PDF (in a browser the button reads Print / Save as PDF). The desktop app saves a paginated PDF straight to a folder you pick, with page numbers on the contents page. In a browser it opens your browser’s print view: choose Save as PDF as the printer. On Android choose Save as PDF, then Download; on iPhone or iPad tap Share (or Print), then Save to Files. The contents page lists every section but without page numbers; the desktop app adds them.
  • Save plan file. On the Documents page, under Your plan. Saves your whole plan (answers, documents and edits) as one file, to keep as a backup or to move to another device.

Your plan is saved on this device. Foundry keeps your answers, documents and edits in this browser, or in the desktop app, so closing the tab or the app does not lose them. It is never uploaded. To carry on somewhere else, tap Save plan file here and Open plan file on the other device. Building again from Instant Build replaces the saved plan, so save a plan file first if you want to keep it.

On a shared computer. A saved plan is removed from the device automatically after 30 days without being opened. To keep nothing at all, tick Don’t keep this plan on this device before you build, or Don’t keep this on this device under Your plan on the Documents page: the plan then lives in that tab only and is gone when you close it.

Questions & fixes

Common questions

What is “Programs you may qualify for”?

An optional last chapter of the Guided Interview (about two minutes) and a screen, Programs you may qualify for, that match you to special programs, certifications and free help: SBA certifications such as 8(a), WOSB, VetCert and HUBZone, national certifications (WBENC, NMSDC, NGLCC, Disability:IN), your state’s DBE and minority- or women-owned certification, SBA Microloans and community lenders, USDA rural programs, and free advising from SBDCs, SCORE, Women’s Business Centers and Veterans Business Outreach Centers, with the nearest office.

Every question is optional and has Prefer not to say; with that, only programs open to everyone are shown. Nothing is guessed from your other answers. Whether you are in a rural area is worked out from your ZIP code’s county, and you are pointed to the official USDA map to confirm. Your answers stay on this device, are never sent anywhere, and never appear in your Business Plan, Financial Statements or pitch. The one exception is your choice: you can list certifications the business already holds as a competitive advantage. Each program lists why it may apply, what it offers, the rules to check and how to apply, with its official source and date. This is not a determination; verify eligibility with the program. Tap Add to my To-Do list on any program, then Update my documents, to put it on your To-Do List.

I don’t know my revenue model yet.

Type “unknown”. The same goes for personal capital (“$0”, “unknown” or “prefer not to say”), location (“not decided”) and outside money (“none” or “unsure”). For location you can also type a ZIP code, a city and state (“Atlanta, GA”) or just the state; the line under the field shows what Foundry read and whose wage figures the plan will use. Missing information only holds back the parts of the pack that depend on it, and they are marked as such.

Where does the revenue in my plan come from?

From your Revenue build-up if you add one: each product or tier with its price, how it is billed (one-time, per month, per year or per unit), the customers or units you expect a month (a start, month 12 and month 36, or a monthly growth rate) and what each unit costs you. It is optional: on Instant Build under Revenue build-up, or later in Complete your plan on the Documents page. With it, every projection, the break-even, the loan coverage and the runway are built from your prices and volumes, and the plan checks them against the market you can reach and against your costs. Without it, revenue is sized from your costs and labeled “implied by costs — not a sales forecast”.

The Documents page says my plan does not work as structured yet.

Foundry sizes the money you need to reach monthly cash break-even, up to twelve months after you open, plus a contingency. It does not borrow to cover years of losses. When the numbers do not break even by then, or cannot carry the loan by Year 2, the plan says so and Paths that could work shows what would change it: each change on its own (price, volume, rent, staffing, your pay, more equity, a longer term) and complete alternatives, each re-run through the same numbers on this device. Build my plan on this track rebuilds every document on one of them and keeps your original plan, so you can go back. These are options to weigh with an adviser, not a promise of funding.

How do I change an answer?

To clarify or add detail, use Adjust notes or Complete your plan on the Documents page; the documents update from what you add. To change one of the nine answers themselves, go back to Instant Build and build again. Instant does not refill the form with your earlier answers, and a new build replaces the saved plan and clears edits made in Documents, so tap Save plan file first if you want to keep it.

Save as PDF does nothing, or the PDF won’t open.

In a browser, Print / Save as PDF opens a new tab to print from. If nothing appears, allow pop-ups for this page and try again. You can also tap Export editable, open the file, and print it from there. The desktop app saves a PDF file directly. If a saved PDF will not open, open it with your device’s built-in viewer (Files on iPhone, Files or Drive on Android).

The file I downloaded opens as a web page.

Exported documents are .html files so they open anywhere. To edit one, open it in Word (File → Open) or upload it to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs. On a phone, look in Files (iPhone) or Downloads (Android).

Documents says “No documents yet”.

Your plan is kept in the browser (or desktop app) on the device where you built it. If you built it somewhere else, open the plan file you saved there with Open plan file. A private or incognito window forgets it when closed, a plan kept in one tab only is gone when that tab closes, a saved plan is removed after 30 days without being opened, and clearing your browser’s site data deletes it; if you have no plan file, build again from Instant Build.

Ask about this plan says no model is available.

Ask answers from your own figures using a model running on this device, such as Ollama. Open Settings in the Ask panel to point it at yours. Without one, the rest of Foundry works normally.

How do I move my plan to another device, or remove it?

On the Documents page, under Your plan, tap Save plan file. Send the file to yourself however you like, then on the other device open Foundry and tap Open plan file. Start a new plan clears this one and takes you to Instant Build; Delete this plan from this device removes it here. Neither touches a plan file you saved. On a shared computer, delete the plan when you are done.

I’m on a shared or public computer. Will the next person see my plan?

Not if you choose Don’t keep this plan on this device on Instant Build (or Don’t keep this on this device under Your plan on the Documents page). The plan, and any edits, then stay in that browser tab only: nothing is written to the device, any copy already saved there is deleted, and closing the tab removes the plan. Save a plan file first if you want to keep it. Without that choice, a saved plan is still removed automatically after 30 days in which nobody opens it; the line under Your plan says which applies. Delete this plan from this device removes it at once.

Is my information sent anywhere?

No. Foundry builds everything on your device. The only time anything leaves it is an email you choose to send us, and it contains only what you write. That includes the Guided Interview: your answers, its voice and its optional follow-ups all stay on this device.

Can I pause the Guided Interview and finish later?

Yes. Every answer is saved as you give it. Tap Pause, or just close the tab, and open the interview again from Documents when you are ready; it picks up at the same question. Review answers lets you change any earlier answer, and Rebuild my documents works at any point, not only at the end.

The document text is small on my phone.

Pinch to zoom works everywhere in Foundry. Wide financial tables scroll sideways inside their own box; swipe the table, not the page.
