What to include in a message

  1. Which app you're using — MaterialLogix Studio or MaterialLogix Foundry.
  2. Your device and platform — for example, Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, or web browser and version.
  3. What you were trying to do, and what happened instead (the exact error message, if any).
  4. The email address connected to your account, if your question is about access or billing.
