Foundry Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026 · MaterialLogix Foundry is a product of LibraSide Technologies, LLC

Your business plan information stays on this device. Foundry does not send it to MaterialLogix or anyone else, and it is never used to train anything. You can delete it at any time.

What's collected

Everything you type into Instant Build, the Guided Interview, or the questions form — your business idea, numbers, answers, notes and any edits you make to your documents — is stored only on your own device (in this browser or in the app), the same way a document on your computer's hard drive is "stored." Foundry does not have a server that receives this information, and there is no MaterialLogix database of founders' business plans.

Files you choose under Choose files on Instant Build are noted (name only) so you can see what you attached; the file contents are not read and never leave your device.

If you use Ask about this plan, your question and the plan's own numbers are sent to a planning engine that runs on your own device — a local model on your computer, or the phone's own on-device model on iOS and Android — and nowhere else. If no on-device engine is available, Ask honestly reports that it cannot reach one; it does not fall back to a cloud service.

The research data update feature, where offered, downloads public reference information (tax rules, licensing fees, published statistics and similar facts) to your device. It does not send anything about you, your business, or your device with the request.

If you purchase a paid edition of Foundry, payment is handled by our payment processor as described under Third-party processors below. If you email us for help, we receive whatever you choose to put in that email, at the address you send it from.

How it's used

Because your business plan information stays on your device, MaterialLogix does not use it for any purpose — not to improve Foundry, not for marketing, and not to train any model. There is nothing on our side to use: we never receive it.

Payment information collected during a purchase is used only to process that purchase and, where applicable, to activate and renew your license, as described below.

If you email admin@materiallogix.com, we use the contents of that message only to answer you or fix the problem you reported.

Third-party processors

Foundry's desktop and browser builds are configured so the app cannot make network requests off your device except to: a planning engine running on your own machine (loopback only — never leaves the device); MaterialLogix's own help site, to open a plain page with no data attached; and a plain email link to admin@materiallogix.com. Anything else is refused. This means there is no analytics vendor, no crash-reporting service, and no advertising network built into Foundry — we don't use them, and the app is not configured to be able to reach them.

Payment processing. Where a paid edition of Foundry is offered, checkout is handled by Stripe, our payment processor, not by Foundry itself — the purchase happens on a separate, secure page and the app never handles or stores your card details. Stripe receives the information needed to process a purchase (such as your email address and payment details) and to tell us that a purchase succeeded, so we can activate your license; it does not receive your business plan information. Stripe's own privacy policy, published at stripe.com, describes how Stripe handles that information; Foundry does not link out to it directly, in keeping with the same egress lockdown described under Security below. Mobile app-store builds of Foundry ship free with no in-app purchase; if that changes, this page will be updated first.

Research packs. Foundry ships with a built-in library of public reference information (tax rules, registration fees, industry benchmarks and similar facts, each with its government or public source cited). Where an in-app update to that library is offered, it is a one-way download: your device asks for the current file and nothing about you, your plan, or your device rides along with that request. Every file is cryptographically signed, and Foundry checks that signature before using anything it downloads; a file that doesn't check out is rejected.

Retention & deletion

  • Business plans and interview answers are kept only on your device, only until you delete them yourself, or until 30 days pass with the plan unopened, at which point Foundry removes it from that device automatically. This is the same schedule for a saved plan and for interview answers in progress.
  • Don't keep this on this device. Choosing this option (on Instant Build, or in the Guided Interview's settings) keeps your plan in that browser tab only; it is never written to durable storage on the device, and it disappears the moment you close the tab. Saving a plan file yourself is the way to keep a copy under this option.
  • Deleting sooner. You can delete a saved plan from your device at any time from within the app. Because MaterialLogix never receives a copy of your business plan information, there is nothing on our side to separately delete.
  • Requests to us. For anything we do hold — such as a support email you sent us, or purchase and license records kept for a paid edition — write to admin@materiallogix.com and we will act on a request to access or delete it within a reasonable time.

Security

Foundry's network access is locked down at the app level, not just described in a policy: the desktop and browser builds are configured to refuse any request off the device other than the short list named under Third-party processors above, whether that request comes from a page, a generated document, or anything else running in the app.

Any file Foundry downloads — such as a research-data update — is cryptographically signed, and the signature is checked before the file is trusted or used. A file that fails that check is rejected outright.

No plan of ours is perfect, and no method of storage on a device is 100% secure, but we've built Foundry so there is no copy of your business plan sitting on a server to be breached in the first place.

Children's privacy

Foundry is a business-planning tool intended for adults starting or running a business. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided information to us directly (for example, in a support email), contact admin@materiallogix.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If how Foundry handles information changes, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top will change. Because this policy describes what the app actually does, a change here means the app changed too — we don't loosen this page without changing the software to match, and we won't quietly widen what leaves your device.

Questions about this policy

MaterialLogix operates Foundry and is the party responsible for this policy. Email admin@materiallogix.com to ask about this policy or about a specific piece of information.
